S. 1727 / H.R. 9792 Employee Ownership Fairness Act

Excludes ESOP stock contributions from the annual caps that otherwise apply to defined-contribution retirement plans, so participants can also fully use a 401(k) alongside their ESOP.

IntroducedCommittee1 Chamber2 ChambersLaw
SenateHouse

Why calling comes first

When your zip resolves, each bill shows a "Call" button before anything else — that's deliberate. Personalized calls and messages measurably outperform generic form campaigns; identical mass emails are largely ignored by congressional staff. The "copy a message" option is there too, but it's meant as a starting draft to personalize, not something to send as-is.
